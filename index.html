Email and a password, or Google, Apple or Facebook. One account opens the app and the website, with the same bookings in both.

Questions

The short answers.

Does booking cost a player anything?

No. You pay the venue’s price for the hour. A free account books just as many courts as a paid one.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. It is still in development, and on neither store. Venues are being signed up first.

What does listing a venue cost?

Nothing to be on it. SportBase takes a share of the bookings it sends you: 5% to 10%, depending on the plan.

What about a walk-in customer?

Yours, entirely. The commission is only ever on a booking that came through the app.

Is my number visible to strangers?

No. Your email and phone are not readable by other users; a venue sees only what it needs to serve your booking.

Can I lock the app?

Yes: a PIN, plus Face ID or a fingerprint where the phone has one. Owner actions that change a price or remove something ask again.

Install

Put SportBase on your home screen.

Not on the App Store or Play Store yet. Here is how to put it on your home screen today. It takes about a minute, and it opens full screen, like any other app.

Your browser can do it from here: one tap, no menu.

  1. 1Open this page in Safari. iPhone only lets Safari do this bit.
  2. 2Tap Share at the bottom, the square with the arrow coming out of it.
  3. 3Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. 4SportBase is now sitting with your other apps, and opens full screen.

Why not an Install button? iPhone does not have one. Apple lets a site install itself only through Share → Add to Home Screen. That is the iPhone install.

  1. 1Open this page in Chrome.
  2. 2Tap Install if Chrome offers it at the bottom of the screen. That is the whole thing.
  3. 3No offer? Tap the three dots at the top right, then Install app, then Install.
  4. 4It lands on your home screen and opens full screen, with no browser bar.

An older Chrome calls the same menu item Add to home screen. Either wording puts SportBase on your phone.

  1. 1In Chrome or Edge, click the small install icon at the right of the address bar, then Install.
  2. 2Or open the browser menu and choose Install SportBase.
  3. 3No icon and no menu item? The site works exactly the same in a normal tab, so bookmark it and you have lost nothing.
