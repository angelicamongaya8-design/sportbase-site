No booking fee, no service charge, no markup on the hour. You see what each court costs, including the venue’s peak rate, before you pick anything.

For venue owners

Inside the app

One download, two apps.

What a player opens and what a venue owner opens are not the same screens. You sign in once, and the app becomes the one you need.

If you play

A map, not a list

Venues near you, filtered by sport and by the hour you actually want, so you can see which court is a ten-minute walk away.

Favourites

The courts you keep going back to, one tap from the home screen.

Several hours at once

Book a block for a long game. Cancelling one hour leaves the rest of the booking standing.

Gear from the venue

Rackets, balls and nets, where the venue rents them. The refundable deposit sits on its own line, never folded into the price.

Promo codes

A venue’s discount comes off the court time — not off a deposit you are getting back anyway.

Tournaments

Open tournaments with the entry fee, the prize pool and the slots left in plain sight. Join with your team.

Reviews with photos

Written by people who actually played there, because the app knows they booked.

Chat, and real support

Message the venue about a booking. Support threads carry a reference number and a stated reply time.

If you run a venue

Courts and rates

Price each court by the hour with a separate peak rate, and take one off the board while the floor is being repaired.

Schedule and closures

Close a holiday or a single hour without cancelling bookings one by one.

Today’s board

Who is coming and when — and a scanner that checks them in from the QR code on their booking.

Earnings

What is expected, what is paid, and what SportBase took, per booking, at the rate agreed the day it was made rather than today’s.

Equipment stock

Stock falls as gear goes out and comes back when it is returned, so the same racket is never rented twice.

Promotions

Run a discount over the days you choose, and it shows up where players are already looking.

Getting listed

Apply with your permit and your venue photos. A person reviews it — nothing goes live unchecked.

Notifications that fit the job

An owner hears about bookings, cancellations and reviews; a player hears about their own game. Separate settings, separately switched off.

Paying

GCash at checkout. The deposit comes back.

Payments run through PayMongo. SportBase never holds your card details.

At booking

The price is locked

Whatever the venue charges the day you book is the price on your booking, even if the venue raises its rate the week after.

On the receipt

Court time, rental, deposit

Three separate lines, never one total. The deposit is held against the gear and returned when you hand it back.

If plans change

Cancel in the app

Say why, and the venue knows straight away instead of holding a court for someone who is not coming. Refunds follow the venue’s own policy.

Questions

The short answers.

Does booking cost a player anything?

No. You pay the venue’s price for the hour. A free account books just as many courts as a paid one.

Can I download it now?

Not yet — it is still in development, and on neither store. Venues are being signed up first.

What does listing a venue cost?

Nothing to be on it. SportBase takes a share of the bookings it sends you: 5% to 10%, depending on the plan.

What about a walk-in customer?

Yours, entirely. The commission is only ever on a booking that came through the app.

Is my number visible to strangers?

No. Your email and phone are not readable by other users; a venue sees only what it needs to serve your booking.

Can I lock the app?

Yes — a PIN, plus Face ID or a fingerprint where the phone has one. Owner actions that change a price or remove something ask again.
