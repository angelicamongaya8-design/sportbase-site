For players

The price you see is the venue’s price.

No booking fee, no service charge, no markup on the hour. You see what each court costs, including the venue’s peak rate, before you pick anything.

Basketball Badminton Padel Tennis Pickleball Volleyball Futsal Table tennis Swimming
01

Pick your hours

Every court at the venue, hour by hour, with what is already taken greyed out. Pick several at once if you want a long game.

02

Add what you need

Rackets, balls and the rest, if the venue rents them. A refundable deposit is shown separately from the rental, never buried in a total.

03

Show the code

Your booking carries a QR code. The venue scans it when you arrive, and that is the check-in — no list at the desk to find your name on.

For venue owners

Listing is free. We take a share of what we bring you.

Nothing to pay to be on SportBase. The commission comes out of bookings SportBase sent you — never out of a customer who walked in on their own.

Plan Per month Commission What it adds
Listed ₱0 10% Findable and bookable, up to 8 photos.
Featured ₱499 7% Above the fold in your city, a place in Nearby Venues, your promotions on the home screen. Pays for itself from about ₱17,000 a month in bookings.
Partner ₱1,499 5% Everything in Featured, a named contact for support, and a monthly report on views, bookings and no-shows. Pays for itself from about ₱50,000 a month in bookings.

And one plan for players

SportBase Plus, ₱129 a month — SportBase gives up its share of your bookings, so you pay the venue’s price and nothing on top. It pays for itself at about three hours a month. Play less than that and the free account is the better deal, which the app says on the page itself.

Where things stand

Not on the App Store yet.

SportBase is being built. Venues are being onboarded now, ahead of the apps going live — if you run courts and want to be on it from the first day, that is the useful thing to do today.
